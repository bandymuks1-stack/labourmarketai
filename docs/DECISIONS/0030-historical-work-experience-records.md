# 0030 — Historical work-experience records (owner decision, 2026-11-04)

Status: **BINDING.** Owner decision, 2026-11-04. It extends decisions
[0020](0020-organization-history-exists-before-claim.md) and
[0025](0025-one-person-one-continuous-professional-history.md). The owner's exact wording, with
its figures, is in the private record; this public file carries no names and no figures.

## Decision

1. The organization-supplied historical records are confirmed company work experience and activity
   history.
2. They are not removed, paused or re-confirmed merely because of their historical origin.
3. They are used to support work experience, professions, competencies, projects and work hours.
4. Public presentation of work experience uses professional facts only. It passes on no names,
   surnames, contacts or other data that identify a person.
5. There is one source of work-hours and experience accounting; no duplicate records are created.
6. A company's internal work history is kept apart from public profiles.
7. No separate GDPR incident is opened merely because of these confirmed historical records.
8. Data that is truly anonymous, and no longer allows a person to be identified, falls outside
   personal-data processing.
9. Where records can still be linked to specific people, the matching access and data-protection
   rules apply.

## What this means in practice

- **The records stay linkable, so rule 9 applies to them.** Every record is tied to a roster person
  with a name as supplied, and the raw source row is kept. They are not anonymous under rule 8.
- An import needs no extra approval or pause because of its age. Decision 0020 stands: the history
  exists before anyone claims it, and a claim needs the person's own confirmation.
- "Confirmed" here describes the company's history. It does not turn a record into independent
  confirmation of a person's skill, and it does not change the evidence states of decisions 0023
  and 0028.
- Decision 0025's reader, `lib/journal/professional-experience.ts`, remains the one total.

## What this does not decide

- The subject's right to refuse an imported record (ARCH-5). A linked person's contest is live; what
  a person without an account can do remains open.
- Notices, processor agreements and retention for these records (gap G-05 in
  `docs/legal/gdpr-2026-10-15/internal/gap-register-2026-10-15.md`). They are ordinary obligations
  under rule 9. They do not stop use of the records.

Findings on access paths are kept in the private record and handled as ordinary fixes.
