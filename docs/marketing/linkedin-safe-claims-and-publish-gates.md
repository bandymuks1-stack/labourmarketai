# Safe Claims & Publish Gates — Labourmarket.ai (binding)

These rules bind ALL LinkedIn marketing material — posts, comments, profile fields, visuals, outreach messages, and these marketing docs themselves. If any other marketing document conflicts with this one, **this document wins**.

## 1. Brand rules

- The public brand is always **Labourmarket.ai** (capital L, lowercase rest, with the dot).
- The legacy five-letter internal codename (starts with "L", ends with "A") must **never** appear in public material — and is not written in these marketing docs either. If you don't know which codename this means, you don't need it; use only "Labourmarket.ai".
- Legal entity, when needed: `Labour Market AI sp. z o.o. — registered in Poland, building for the European labour market.`

## 2. Positioning rules

- **Never construction-only.** Construction is the *first vertical*; the platform is universal. Every time construction is mentioned as the focus, the universal design must be stated or clearly implied.
- **Recruitment/staffing agencies are a core pillar — never an enemy.** No "cut out the middleman" framing, no agency-bashing, not even as a joke or engagement bait. We build tools *for* agencies.
- **Poland is the legal seat, Europe is the market.** Say both. Never imply the market is limited to Poland, or to Lithuania, or to any single country. Launch focus markets: LT, LV, EE, NL, DE, DK, NO, SE, PL — listed as focus, never as the boundary of ambition.
- The platform is **human-first**: the person is the spine (one account, all roles). AI is assistance with traceable evidence, never a black box that judges humans.

## 3. Honesty rules (hard bans)

Never, in any channel:

- **No fake followers** — no purchased or bot followers, no engagement pods, no implying audience size.
- **No fake clients/customers** — no naming, hinting at, or visually implying customers that don't exist. No "trusted by" walls until real, permission-granted customers exist.
- **No fake workers** — no invented worker profiles, testimonials, or success stories. Real stories require the person's explicit consent.
- **No fake metrics** — no invented or extrapolated numbers (users, matches, placements, growth, revenue). If we can't prove a number from real data the owner can verify, it does not get published. Asking questions ("what % in your experience?") is fine; asserting numbers is not.
- **No fake verified claims** — "verified" is a product state earned through the real verification ladder; never describe anything as verified in marketing unless it is verified in the product.
- **No fake live marketplace** — never imply that matching, hiring, or transactions are live and humming if they are not. Concept visuals of unshipped features must be visibly labelled "concept" or "not live yet".
- **The banned product word.** The four-letter word spelled d‑e‑m‑o (in any casing, in any language, including compounds built on it) is banned from all product and marketing copy — and from these marketing docs. Talk about the product's real state instead, using the allowed phrases below. This mirrors the product-copy guard already enforced in the repo.

## 4. Allowed status phrases (the only ones)

When describing the product's state, use exactly these:

- "early product build"
- "in progress"
- "not live yet"
- "preparing first customers"

Anything stronger ("launched", "live", "operating", "growing fast") requires the thing to be literally true and owner-confirmed before use.

## 5. Publish gates (hard process rules)

- **Draft-only by default.** Every post, comment template, profile change, and outreach message is a draft until the owner approves it.
- **Posts:** the owner approves the **exact final text** (and exact final visual). Any change after approval re-enters the gate.
- **Outreach:** the owner approves the **exact recipient AND the exact message, as a pair**. Approval never transfers to a different recipient or a reworded message. Sending is always a manual owner action.
- **No automation past the gate:** no auto-publishing, no scheduled auto-posting, no auto-messaging, no auto-commenting, no auto-connecting. No scraping of LinkedIn; no login automation, captcha solving, or rate-limit bypassing — ever.
- Silence is not approval. There is no timeout that turns a draft into a published post.
- If everything is ready and only the owner's manual publish action remains, the status to report is:
  `BLOCKED_FOR_PUBLISH_ONLY — drafts ready, publishing requires owner action.`

## 6. Pre-publish self-check (run on every draft)

1. Brand written only as "Labourmarket.ai"? Legacy codename absent?
2. Construction framed as first vertical, not the whole product?
3. Agencies framed as a core pillar?
4. Poland-as-seat AND Europe-as-market both clear? No single-country framing?
5. Zero invented numbers, customers, workers, or verified claims? No live-marketplace implication?
6. The banned d‑e‑m‑o word absent (case-insensitive, all languages)?
7. Status language only from the allowed list in §4?
8. Owner has approved this exact text (and exact recipient, for outreach)?

All eight must pass. Otherwise the draft goes back, not out.
