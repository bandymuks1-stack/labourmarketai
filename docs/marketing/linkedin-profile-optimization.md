## About (ready to paste)

```
The labour market is chaotic. Workers can't prove they are ready. Companies can't see who is available, documented and suitable. Staffing agencies need structure, trust and visibility.

Labourmarket.ai is a human-first labour market operating system. It connects workers, companies and staffing agencies around one simple spine: the person. One account, all roles — a worker today can be a company owner or an agency operator tomorrow, without losing their history.

What the platform is built around:
• Verified skills — declared, then backed by daily work-journal entries and manager-confirmed work proofs
• Documents and compliance readiness — who is actually allowed and prepared to work
• Real labour demand — structured customer requests instead of guesswork
• Agencies as a core pillar — worker pools with structure and visibility, not a workaround

Our first vertical is construction, but the platform is universal — the same trust chain works for any trade or profession.

We are built for the whole European region, with launch focus on Lithuania, Latvia, Estonia, the Netherlands, Germany, Denmark, Norway, Sweden and Poland.

Status: early product build. We are honest about what works today and what is still in progress — no inflated claims, ever.

Labour Market AI sp. z o.o. — registered in Poland, building for the European labour market.
```

## Banner direction

- **Text on banner (max one line):** "The human-first labour market operating system — built for Europe."
- **Do not** put numbers, logos of companies that aren't customers, or anything implying a live marketplace on the banner.
- Keep it legible at mobile crop (center-weighted composition).

## Comment & reply style

- Reply as a builder, not a marketer: specific, short, generous with reasoning.
- Disagree politely and with evidence; never dunk on staffing agencies or competitors — agencies are a core pillar of the platform's worldview.
- When asked "is it live?": answer honestly with an allowed status phrase ("early product build", "in progress", "not live yet", "preparing first customers") and say what concretely works today.
- Never argue about claims we can't evidence. If unsure whether a feature claim is current, check before replying — or don't claim it.
- No engagement bait, no fake controversy, no tagging strangers for reach.

## Safe claim rules (summary — full rules in the safe-claims doc)

- Brand is always **Labourmarket.ai**.
- Construction is the **first vertical**, never the whole product.
- Recruitment/staffing agencies are a **core pillar**, never framed as the enemy.
- **Poland is the legal seat, Europe is the market** — say both; never imply the market is limited to Poland or to Lithuania.
- No fake followers, clients, workers, metrics, or verified-status claims. No fake live marketplace.
- Status language only from the allowed list above.
