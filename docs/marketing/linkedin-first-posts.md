## Post 1 — Launch: the founder narrative

```
The labour market is chaotic. I'm building an operating system for it.

Here's what that chaos actually looks like, from three sides:

Workers can't prove they're ready. A great carpenter with 15 years of experience often has nothing but a thin CV and a stack of photos on an old phone. Skill exists — proof doesn't.

Companies can't see who is available, documented and suitable. Demand is real and urgent — "we need four people on site, Monday" — but turning that sentence into the right humans, with valid documents, is guesswork.

Staffing agencies sit in the middle and need structure, trust and visibility. They are not the problem. They're a core pillar of how labour actually moves across Europe — and they deserve real tools, not spreadsheets and phone tag.

So we're building Labourmarket.ai: a human-first labour market operating system.

The spine is the person. One account, every role — worker, company, agency, customer. Around the person, a trust chain: declared skills → daily work-journal entries → manager-confirmed work proofs → trust that is portable and belongs to the human, not to any employer.

We start with construction as the first vertical, because the pain is sharpest there. But the platform is universal — the same trust chain works for any trade.

And we're building for the whole European region, with launch focus on Lithuania, Latvia, Estonia, the Netherlands, Germany, Denmark, Norway, Sweden and Poland.

Honest status: early product build. No inflated claims — that's a rule we apply to our marketing as strictly as to our product.

The goal is simple to say and hard to build:
Who is ready. What is missing. Who needs people. What should happen next.

If you're a worker, a builder, or an agency operator anywhere in Europe — follow along. And if you're hiring in construction, message us: we are preparing first customers.

Labour Market AI sp. z o.o. — registered in Poland, building for the European labour market.
```

## Post 2 — The trust chain (for workers, 2–4 days after Post 1)

```
A CV is a claim. We're building evidence.

Think about how trust in a worker actually forms on a real site:

Day 1: they say what they can do.
Week 2: the work starts speaking for itself.
Month 3: the site manager would vouch for them without hesitation.

Then the project ends — and all of that earned trust evaporates. The next employer starts from the CV again.

That's the core injustice we're fixing at Labourmarket.ai, and the mechanism is deliberately simple:

1. Declared skills — the worker states what they can do.
2. Work journal — short daily entries: what was done, where.
3. Work proofs — a manager confirms the entries. One tap, real accountability.
4. Portable trust — the confirmed record belongs to the person and travels with them, employer to employer, country to country.

No black-box scores. No magic badges. Just claims, evidence, and confirmation — visible to whoever the worker chooses to show.

We're starting in construction, building for the whole European region.

Status, honestly: in progress. The trust chain is the heart of the product and we'd rather build it right than fast.

Workers: what's the hardest thing about proving what you can do? I'm reading every comment.
```

## Post 3 — Demand side: companies and agencies (about a week after Post 1)

```
"We need four bricklayers in Rotterdam, starting Monday."

One sentence. Profession, count, place, date. And yet, almost everywhere in Europe, turning it into four real, documented, ready people is days of phone calls and hope.

Why? Because the demand side of the labour market runs on unstructured noise:

— Companies post ads into the void and can't see who is actually available, documented and suitable.
— Staffing agencies — the people who move most of Europe's flexible workforce — manage their worker pools in spreadsheets, with trust stored in one recruiter's head.
— Workers who ARE ready are indistinguishable from those who aren't.

At Labourmarket.ai we treat demand as structured data, not prose: a customer request states the profession, skills, location and dates — so it can be matched against people whose readiness is backed by evidence (verified skills, document status, confirmed work history), not just keywords.

And to be very clear about one thing: we're building tools FOR staffing agencies, not around them. Agencies are a core pillar of this platform. A structured, evidence-backed worker pool makes a good agency more valuable, not less.

Construction first. Universal by design. Built for Europe — launch focus on LT, LV, EE, NL, DE, DK, NO, SE and PL.

Honest status: preparing first customers.

If you run construction projects or a staffing agency and this sounds like your Monday — message us. The earliest conversations shape what gets built.
```
