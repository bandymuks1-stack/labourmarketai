# Labourmarket.ai — pilot offer (EN)

**Pilot release.** Not the final product, not a marketplace yet. One honest line per topic for first testers and companies.

## What we offer at this stage

| | |
|---|---|
| **What it is** | A labour-market OS: a place where a worker describes what they do in plain text, and the system helps turn that into clean skills + a work-evidence stream. Later, companies and agencies can see real proof of what a worker can do — not just a CV. |
| **What works today** | Sign-in (Google), profile/CV with free-text + skill suggestions, work journal (Lithuanian text → work fragments confirmed by the worker), language reports (testers flag confusing copy), private drafts for company / agency / buyer roles, internal conversations between tester and admin. |
| **What's NOT here yet** | Real matching between workers and openings. Public job postings. "Verified by manager" markers (the manager-confirmation layer is still being prepared). Real-time notifications. Mobile app. Payments. |
| **Pricing** | Pilot phase — free. Paid tier ships only when the foundation is reliable AND companies provide real organisational details (rekvizitai). |

## What a tester-worker gets

- A clean personal profile / CV with self-declared skills.
- A way to log daily work in plain language (free text → structure).
- Later — the ability to show that evidence to real employers as "here's what I actually do".
- Real influence on how the platform shapes itself for the industry.

## What a tester-company gets

- A first private draft for "what worker / team / service we're looking for".
- A view of how the first worker profiles will look (real CV + journal shape).
- A channel to comment on copy (what's confusing, what's unclear).
- A direct channel with the platform owner (internal conversations).
- **NOT a guarantee** that a worker will appear today.

## Honest limits

- **Privacy:** your text is visible only to you + the platform admin. No hidden tracking. No screen recording. No keystroke capture. Telemetry (task duration) doesn't include your text.
- **Security:** Google sign-in + RLS at the database. No `service_role` in app runtime. No public endpoints into your data.
- **Bugs:** it's a pilot. Things can break. Tell us — usually fixed within 24h.
- **We don't fake what isn't built.** If you see "verified" anywhere, it'll be **real** human confirmation (and since that layer doesn't ship yet, you won't see any "verified" markers).

## How to start

1. We send you a personal invite + a short testing script.
2. It takes 20–30 minutes.
3. After: 1–2 days for you to decide if you'd come back a week later.
4. Your notes go into the next release.

## What's on the longer horizon

- Real matching based on **proven** experience, not just CV.
- Manager / client confirmation layer for journal entries.
- Company rekvizitai + verification status → only then serial sales.
- Team management as sports-team style: bench, readiness, project fit.
- Paid tier once the foundation is proven with real users.
