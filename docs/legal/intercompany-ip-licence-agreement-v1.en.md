## § 3. Licence Grant and Scope

3.1. The Licensor grants the Licensee a **non-transferable** licence to use the Licensed IP for **commercial purposes**, namely the operation, marketing, and commercial exploitation of the Platform.

3.2. **Territory:** the European Union and the European Economic Area (EU/EEA).

3.3. **Duration:** the licence is granted for the First Licence Period (the first twelve (12) months from the Agreement Effective Date) and thereafter continues under and subject to the terms of this Agreement (including § 8 and § 11) until this Agreement is terminated in accordance with its terms.

3.4. **Permitted downstream rights.** The Licensee may grant to its end users, customers, and technical suppliers (e.g. hosting and infrastructure providers) **only the limited usage rights necessary for the provision and use of the Platform as a service**. Such downstream rights must not exceed what is needed to use or support the Platform and must terminate no later than the underlying user/customer/supplier relationship.

3.5. **Prohibitions.** The Licensee shall **not**:

   (a) sell, assign, or otherwise dispose of the Licensed IP or any part of it;

   (b) register any element of the Licensed IP (including the brand, name, domains, or designs) in its own name or in the name of any third party;

   (c) sublicense the Licensed IP more broadly than permitted by § 3.4 without the Licensor's prior written consent;

   (d) use the Licensed IP for products or activities unrelated to the Platform.

3.6. The licence is non-exclusive unless the Parties agree otherwise in writing [VERIFY-BEFORE-SIGNING: the Parties and their advisers should expressly decide exclusive vs non-exclusive; this draft does not decide it].

## § 4. Improvements

4.1. **"Platform Improvement"** means any new code, design, documentation, data structure, functionality, or other work that modifies, extends, or is derived from the Licensed IP or is created specifically for the Platform, whether created by the Licensee's employees, the Licensee's contractors, the Licensor, or jointly.

4.2. **Development costs.** Unless otherwise agreed in writing for a specific work package, the Party that commissions or performs the development bears its own development costs. Costs borne by the Licensee that qualify as direct platform operating costs are taken into account in the Adjusted Net Profit calculation under Schedule B.

4.3. **Ownership and transfer.** The Parties intend that the **economic (proprietary) rights** in Platform Improvements are consolidated with the Licensor, so that the Licensed IP remains a single, coherent bundle of rights. To that end:

   (a) where Platform Improvements are created by or for the Licensee, the Licensee shall procure that all transferable economic rights in them are transferred to the Licensor, by written instruments satisfying the form required by applicable law;

   (b) the commercial terms of each such transfer (including any consideration) shall be documented in writing [VERIFY-BEFORE-SIGNING: tax and transfer-pricing advisers must review the consideration mechanism before signing];

   (c) each transfer is recorded in a written amendment to Schedule A.

4.4. **Licensee's retained usage right.** Upon and after any transfer under § 4.3, the transferred Improvements automatically form part of the Licensed IP and the Licensee retains the licence rights in them under § 3, without any additional fee beyond the Monthly Licence Fee.

4.5. **No deemed transfer.** **No transfer of economic rights is deemed to occur under this Agreement by conduct, payment, or implication.** A transfer occurs only where a written instrument satisfying the form required by the applicable law (including, where applicable, the written-form requirements of copyright law) has been signed. Until such an instrument is signed for a given Improvement, the creating Party retains its rights and the other Party has only such rights as the law and any existing written agreements give it.

## § 7. Licence Fee

7.1. **First Licence Period.** This § 7 sets out the fee model for the **First Licence Period**, i.e. the first twelve (12) consecutive calendar months from the Agreement Effective Date, and continues to apply thereafter in accordance with § 8.4.

7.2. **Monthly Licence Fee.** For each calendar month, the Monthly Licence Fee equals:

> **Monthly Licence Fee = 30% × Positive Adjusted Net Profit from LabourMarket.ai Activity for the previous calendar month.**

   (a) If the Adjusted Net Profit for the previous calendar month is **positive**, the fee is 30% of that amount.

   (b) If the Adjusted Net Profit is **zero**, the fee is **0 EUR**.

   (c) If the Adjusted Net Profit is **negative**, the fee is **0 EUR** (no negative fee, no credit, and no carry-forward of losses against future months unless the Parties agree otherwise in writing).

   (d) The fee is **never computed on the whole of UAB „Nonstop Group“'s business**; it is computed **only** on the results attributable to LabourMarket.ai activity, as segregated under Schedule B.

7.3. **Adjusted Net Profit — summary definition.** (Full detail: **Schedule B**, which prevails in case of doubt as to computation mechanics.) Adjusted Net Profit for a calendar month means:

   (a) **revenues attributed to LabourMarket.ai activity** for that month — net of VAT, minus credit notes, refunds, chargebacks, and discounts, recognised per the Licensee's revenue-recognition rules;

   **MINUS**

   (b) direct platform operating costs; payment collection costs; hosting and infrastructure costs; platform-attributed marketing costs; employee and contractor costs directly related to the platform; customer support costs; and a reasonable, consistently-allocated share of overheads;

   **BUT calculated BEFORE (i.e. without deducting):**

   (c) that month's licence fee itself (no circularity); corporate income tax; dividends and profit distributions; financing costs unrelated to the platform; shareholders' personal expenses; and unjustified one-off or non-operating costs.

7.4. **Process.**

   (a) The Licensee's accounting function calculates the Adjusted Net Profit using a **consistent monthly methodology** based on the prior month's accounting and management reports (Schedule B).

   (b) A **monthly licence-fee calculation statement** is prepared **by the 10th business day of the following month** and provided to both companies.

   (c) Errors discovered later are corrected in the **next month's statement** (no restatement of already-issued invoices unless accountants determine a correction invoice is mandatory).

   (d) The statement must **not contain unnecessary personal data** of employees or customers.

   (e) The Licensor issues its invoice on the basis of the statement.

   (f) **Payment term:** within **fourteen (14) calendar days** of the invoice date, unless the Parties' accountants determine that a different term is mandatory under applicable rules.

   (g) **Currency:** EUR.

7.5. **Invoice description.** Each invoice shall describe the fee substantially as follows (in the invoice language):

> "Monthly licence fee for the commercial use of the LabourMarket.ai software, brand and related intellectual property under Licence Agreement No. [NUMBER], calculated as 30% of the Adjusted Net Profit from LabourMarket.ai activity for [MONTH/YEAR]."

7.6. **Forbidden invoice wording.** Invoices and statements under this Agreement must **not** describe the fee as, or use the words: **"dividend"**, **"profit distribution"**, **"author payment"** (authorial remuneration), **"salary"**, or **"management fee"**. The fee is consideration for the licence of intellectual property and nothing else.

## § 8. Twelve-Month Review of the Fee Model

8.1. **No later than thirty (30) days before the end of the First Licence Period**, the Parties shall begin a joint review of the fee model.

8.2. The review shall consider at least: actual revenues; actual costs; profitability of the Platform; the value of the Licensed IP; and the functions performed and risks borne by each Party.

8.3. Following the review, the Parties may agree **in writing** to: (a) keep the 30% rate; (b) change the percentage; (c) move to a fixed fee; or (d) adopt a mixed model.

8.4. **No retroactive change** to the fee for past months may be made without both Parties' express written agreement. **If no new written agreement is signed in time, the existing formula in § 7 continues to apply** until a written amendment is signed or this Agreement is terminated in accordance with its terms.
