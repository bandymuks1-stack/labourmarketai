# Licence Fee Accounting Policy — LabourMarket.ai Activity (v1)

> tax adviser. It records the intended internal methodology and must be

> accountants before the first monthly statement and first invoice are issued.

Applies to: UAB „Nonstop Group“ (operator/licensee, prepares the statement)
and Labour Market AI Sp. z o.o. (licensor, receives the statement and
invoices the fee).

Effective from: [AGREEMENT EFFECTIVE DATE] (draft — not yet in force)

## 1. Purpose

Define one consistent, documented, repeatable monthly methodology for
computing **Adjusted Net Profit from LabourMarket.ai activity** and the
resulting **Monthly Licence Fee (30% of positive Adjusted Net Profit; 0 EUR if
zero or negative)** under the intercompany licence agreement.

## 2. Core principles

1. **Consistency.** The same attribution and allocation rules are applied
   every month. Any change to the methodology requires a written, dated
   revision of this policy, applied prospectively, and disclosed on the next
   statement.
2. **Direct assignment first.** Every revenue and cost item that can be
   directly and objectively traced to LabourMarket.ai activity is assigned
   directly. Allocation keys are used only where direct assignment is
   impossible.
3. **Documented allocation for shared costs.** Each shared cost category has
   one named allocation key, recorded in this policy, with a short written
   justification. Keys are reviewed at the 12-month review.
4. **Alignment with statutory accounting.** Revenue recognition in the
   statement follows the same recognition principles used in UAB „Nonstop
   Group“'s statutory bookkeeping (accrual basis; the applicable Lithuanian
   accounting framework as confirmed by the accountant). The statement must
   reconcile to the statutory ledgers.
5. **No circularity.** Adjusted Net Profit is computed BEFORE deducting the
   licence fee itself and before corporate income tax.
6. **Honesty of exclusions.** Dividends, non-platform financing costs,
   shareholder personal expenses, and unjustified one-off items are excluded
   and, where material, listed in the statement's notes.
7. **No personal data.** Statements contain only aggregated financial figures.
   No employee, worker, or customer personal data (names, identifiers,
   individual salaries, individual customer invoices identifiable to a
   natural person) may appear in a statement.

## 3. Revenue attribution

Attributed revenue = revenue from LabourMarket.ai platform activity,
recognized per §2.4, measured as:

- gross platform-attributed invoiced revenue,
- **minus** VAT,
- **minus** credit notes,
- **minus** refunds,
- **minus** chargebacks,
- **minus** discounts granted,

= **Net Attributed Revenue** for the month.

Attribution rule: revenue is platform-attributed if the underlying customer
contract or invoice line relates to LabourMarket.ai platform services. Mixed
invoices are split at line level; if a line genuinely cannot be split, the
accountant documents the basis used and applies it consistently.

## 4. Cost attribution

### 4.1 Directly assigned cost categories

- Direct platform costs (platform-specific software, tools, services).
- Payment collection costs (payment processor fees on platform revenue).
- Hosting and infrastructure for the platform.
- Platform-attributed marketing (campaigns identifiably for LabourMarket.ai).
- Employee/contractor costs directly related to platform work (time
  identifiably spent on the platform; supported by contracts, invoices, or
  time records — aggregated in the statement, never per-person detail).
- Customer support for platform customers.

### 4.2 Shared / overhead costs

A **reasonable, consistently applied share of overhead** is allocated using
documented keys. Initial keys (to be confirmed by the accountant):

| Shared cost | Allocation key (initial) | Justification |
|---|---|---|
| General accounting/admin services | Proportion of platform revenue to total company revenue, or fixed documented % if revenue proportion is unrepresentative in early months | Simple, auditable |
| Shared software/subscriptions | Documented usage share | Reflects actual use |
| Premises/utilities (if any shared) | Headcount-time share of platform-related work | Reflects resource use |

The key actually used each month is stated on the statement. Changing a key
mid-year requires a written policy revision (§2.1).

### 4.3 Excluded items (never deducted)

- The licence fee itself (no circularity).
- Corporate income tax.
- Dividends and profit distributions.
- Financing costs unrelated to the platform.
- Shareholder personal expenses.
- Unjustified one-off items (a one-off may be included only with a written
  justification in the statement notes).

## 5. Statement preparation and review

- **Prepared by:** UAB „Nonstop Group“ accounting (the operator holds the
  books of record for platform activity).
- **Deadline:** by the 10th business day of the month following the reporting
  calendar month.
- **Distribution:** the statement is delivered to BOTH companies (operator
  management and licensor management) at the same time.
- **Invoicing:** Labour Market AI Sp. z o.o. issues its licence-fee invoice
  based on the statement; payment within 14 calendar days; currency EUR.
- **Review:** the licensor may query any line; queries are answered with
  supporting documentation (aggregated, no personal data).

## 6. Corrections

Errors discovered after a statement is issued are **corrected in the next
month's statement** as a separately identified correction line (positive or
negative), with a one-line explanation. Corrections do not reopen already
issued invoices unless the accountants of both companies confirm in writing
that a credit note is required instead.

## 7. Documentation retention and audit trail

- Each monthly statement, its underlying calculation workbook/export, the
  allocation-key evidence, and the resulting invoice are retained together as
  one monthly evidence pack.
- Retention period: per the statutory accounting document retention rules of
  Lithuania (operator) and Poland (licensor) — **the exact periods must be
  confirmed by each company's accountant; do not assume**. Retain at minimum
  for as long as either country's rules require, and never less than the
  duration of any open tax audit window.
- The audit trail must allow a third party (auditor, tax inspector) to go
  from the invoice → statement → ledger entries → source documents without
  gaps.

## 8. Open items for the accountants (confirm IN WRITING before first statement)

1. Confirmation that the revenue-recognition alignment in §2.4 matches the
   statutory framework actually applied by UAB „Nonstop Group“.
2. Confirmation (or replacement) of each allocation key in §4.2.
3. Confirmation of retention periods in §7 under current LT and PL rules.
4. VAT treatment of the licence-fee invoice (see
   `cross-border-tax-review-checklist-v1.md`) — MUST BE CONFIRMED IN WRITING
   BEFORE the first invoice.
5. Whether any withholding applies to the fee payment and the evidence needed
   before the first payment.
