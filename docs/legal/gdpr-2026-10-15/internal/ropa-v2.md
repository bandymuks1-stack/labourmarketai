## 2. Baseline technical and organisational measures (Art. 30(1)(g), 32)

This section summarises the baseline; details are in `security-and-incident-procedures-v1.md`.

- **Access control.** Fail-closed RLS on every personal table. `can_view_worker` routes visibility through consent and accepted relationships. Superadmin gates protect operator surfaces. Consent RPCs derive the subject from `auth.uid()`, never from a parameter.
- **Integrity.** Append-only and immutable triggers on ledgers and event tables (24 tables). Hash chains on journals, assistant messages and evidence records.
- **Minimisation.** No IP or user agent in the consent ledger. Telemetry metadata allowlist. Allowed-field payload policy for AI tasks. Default-deny AI egress gate.
- **Storage.** All 6 buckets are private, with signed short-lived URLs.
- **Known weaknesses** (gap register): no erasure executor; no evidenced DR; service-role key in CI; non-EEA edge middleware; undisclosed analytics storage.
