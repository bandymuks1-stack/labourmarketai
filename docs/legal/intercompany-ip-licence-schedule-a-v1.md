## A.2 Exclusions — not licensed under this Agreement

1. **Open-source components and third-party components.** All open-source software (libraries, frameworks, runtimes, build tools) and other third-party components incorporated in or used by the Platform are **excluded**. They remain governed exclusively by their own licence terms (e.g. MIT, Apache-2.0, GPL-family, BSD, proprietary SaaS terms). The Licensee is responsible for its own compliance with those licences in operating the Platform. Nothing in the Agreement or this Schedule purports to sublicense them.
2. **Third-party fonts, icon sets, stock images, and purchased design assets** — remain under their own licences.
3. **Third-party services and APIs** (hosting, database-as-a-service, payment, e-mail, analytics providers) — contractual rights sit with whichever company is the account holder; not licensed here.
4. **Personal data and database contents.** No personal data, CVs, contacts, uploaded documents, or production database content are licensed, transferred, or made accessible under the Agreement (Agreement § 5).
5. **Any right the Licensor does not actually hold.** Where the Chain-of-Title Audit shows a gap, the corresponding item is licensed only to the extent of the Licensor's actual rights (Agreement § 2.3–2.4).

## A.3 Amendments

Additions or changes to this inventory (including recording future Improvements under Agreement § 4) are valid only as written amendments to this Schedule signed by both Parties (Agreement § 11.5). Each amendment should state: item description, creation/transfer date, transfer instrument reference, and updated chain-of-title status.
