# Living labour market visual grammar

This grammar applies to the isolated owner-review surface at
`/[locale]/living-labour-market`. It is not permission to change the frozen
production landing.

## The world is the navigation

- Show recognizable workplaces, workers, tools, vehicles and work rhythms.
- Use one continuous terrain from market scale to task scale; do not stack
  conventional landing sections.
- Roads represent real movement through an economy. Amber activity represents
  an illustrative work process. Cyan beacons represent public vacancy supply.
- A selected profession changes the camera target, workplace emphasis, demand
  intensity and real opportunity destination together.

## Scale sequence

1. Market: all work sectors operate simultaneously over the European terrain.
2. Need: public profession supply becomes a beacon at its workplace.
3. Person: a worker moves toward the work rather than appearing as a profile card.
4. Work: the camera closes on people, tools and a progressing task.
5. Evidence: the implemented Work Journal model appears as a physical work record.
6. Decision: approval and capacity radiate back into the same workplace.
7. Market: the camera returns to the economy with the completed signal visible.

## Truth boundary

- Current counts and job titles come only from the existing anonymous public
  vacancy projection.
- Sector positions, worker movement, construction progress and process timing are
  illustrative choreography and are labelled as such.
- No worker records, employer identities or vacancy coordinates are exposed.
- When the live reader fails, show the governed measured floor and unavailable
  sector counts; never manufacture a live value.

## Reuse on product surfaces

- Preserve the amber/cyan truth distinction and the need-to-evidence sequence.
- Reuse individual world primitives near their real product destination: demand
  at jobs, work progress at projects, evidence at Journal, confirmation at
  approvals, and aggregate capacity at planning.
- Do not copy the full cinematic shell into dashboards. Product pages remain
  operational surfaces; the grammar supplies context, motion and continuity.

## Runtime limits

- Canvas 2D only, capped at 1.5 device-pixel ratio and 30 frames per second.
- Pause when hidden or offscreen; render once under reduced motion.
- Ship no raster/video world asset and add no runtime dependency for this route.
