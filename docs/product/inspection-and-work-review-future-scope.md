# Inspection & Work Review — Future Scope

> **Future scope, not an active claim.** Nothing here works today. Do not state
> these as live capabilities in any public copy. Grounded in
> [`responsibility-matrix.md`](./responsibility-matrix.md) and
> [`ai-recommendation-disclaimer.md`](./ai-recommendation-disclaimer.md).

## 1. Intent (future)

Future oversight services that could help confirm work progress and reduce risk
before acceptance:

- **document-status review** by a human / operational partner;
- **AI-assisted remote analysis** of work / project progress from photos / video
  (assistive signal only, never a verdict of record);
- **human work review**;
- **on-site inspection** before work acceptance;
- reputation and reliability signals fed by real, evidenced events;
- contractual **work-order** management;
- accommodation / travel coordination;
- a future payments / deposit path.

## 2. Hard rules

- All of the above are **future** — never advertised as already working.
- AI remains assistive (drafts / signals), never the authority that accepts work
  or verifies documents.
- Any future build is owner-gated; DB-touching parts ship as additive, reversible,
  human-gated migrations (no migration in this scope).
- **No live payments** are enabled by this scope; the payments/deposit path is a
  separate, deliberate, owner-gated effort.
- No fake inspections, no fabricated work-review results.
