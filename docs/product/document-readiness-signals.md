# Document-Readiness Signals

> What "document-readiness signals" mean — and what they deliberately do **not**
> mean. Grounded in [`responsibility-matrix.md`](./responsibility-matrix.md).

## 1. What a readiness signal IS

A **signal** computed from **declared** document data (which documents a person/
company says they have, expiry dates, target country) that indicates what is
**likely still missing or expiring** before work can start. It helps reduce risk
by making gaps visible early.

## 2. What a readiness signal is NOT

It is **not**:
- a confirmation that a document is **genuine** or **legally valid**;
- a **verification** of the person, their skills, or their legal right to work;
- **legal advice**.

The platform never reads document file contents to assert facts (there is no such
pipeline today), and the AI helper never sets a verification status.

## 3. Honest wording

Use: "document-readiness signal", "likely required", "needs verification", "not
legal advice". Never: "verified documents", "legally verified", "guaranteed",
"document is genuine".

## 4. Who owns the truth

Document authenticity and legal validity are owned by the **issuer** and the
relevant **authority**, confirmed where applicable by the employer / a human
reviewer — never asserted by the platform or the AI. See
[`ai-recommendation-disclaimer.md`](./ai-recommendation-disclaimer.md).
