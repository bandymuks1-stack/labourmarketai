# General Labour-Market Platform Principle

> The binding principle for how Labourmarket.ai positions itself, publicly and
> internally. It governs the landing copy, the value blocks, and every public
> surface. Companion docs: [`public-trust-positioning.md`](./public-trust-positioning.md),
> [`platform-vs-operating-company-responsibility.md`](./platform-vs-operating-company-responsibility.md),
> [`partner-and-supply-safety-model.md`](./partner-and-supply-safety-model.md).

## 1. What Labourmarket.ai is

**Labourmarket.ai is a general labour-market platform** — shared infrastructure
that helps workers, employers, agencies and partners work more clearly, more
transparently, and more reliably. **It is not a construction-only project**, not a
demo, and not a closed intermediary model. It serves **all sectors**.

The platform structures: worker profiles, company needs, document-readiness
signals, reputation, agreements, partner reliability, and cooperation history — so
that accurate profiles, clear needs, document-readiness signals, fair agreements
and reputation **reduce risk for every market participant**.

## 2. Who can participate

Workers · employers · recruitment agencies · staffing / temporary-work companies ·
subcontractors · brigades · sector partners · operational partners · and (future)
document / oversight / mediation partners. The platform is **open to all sectors**:
construction, manufacturing, logistics, hospitality, agriculture, cleaning, care,
technical, and others.

## 3. What the platform is NOT (by itself)

By itself — unless explicitly set in a separate contract or through a separate
operating company — the platform is **not**: an automatic employer, a document
guarantor, a legal representative, a licensed employment agency, or a guarantor of
work quality. AI is a **helper layer** for drafts / recommendations / signals —
**never** a legal, document, employment, or final-decision authority.

## 4. Public framing rules (binding)

- **Do** speak about: trust, transparency, fair dealing, reputation,
  document-readiness, and risk reduction; "general labour-market platform";
  "reliable CV / clearer profiles"; "trusted cooperation".
- **Do NOT** publicly frame the product as construction-only; do **not** say
  "construction clients go through our company" or "we are the intermediary in
  construction" or "everyone must go through us"; do **not** use demo / fake /
  mock / sample / preview-only framing as the landing's character; do **not**
  over-claim "skill verification" as the core public product; do **not** promise
  legal/document verification, a guaranteed worker, or a guaranteed match.

## 5. Internal safety logic (kept out of public copy)

Internally, the product **may** route unverified or risky companies through a safe
supply path so they cannot get uncontrolled direct access to client or worker
contacts until reviewed — operating through an approved responsible partner or our
operational route in the meantime. **Public copy never phrases this as "everyone
goes through us"** — it is expressed only as a principle of trust, transparency,
reputation and a fair market. See
[`partner-and-supply-safety-model.md`](./partner-and-supply-safety-model.md).
