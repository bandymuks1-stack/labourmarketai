# Platform vs Operating Company — Responsibility Split

> Separates the responsibilities of the **Labourmarket.ai platform**, a separate
> **operating company**, **partners/agencies**, **employers**, **workers**, the
> **AI helper layer**, and **future** mediation/oversight/document-review
> services. Companion: [`responsibility-matrix.md`](./responsibility-matrix.md).

## 1. The parties

- **Labourmarket.ai platform** — shared infrastructure that structures profiles,
  needs, document-readiness signals, reputation, agreements, partner reliability
  and cooperation history.
- **Operating company** — a separate legal entity that may, under explicit
  contract, take on operational roles (e.g. acting as a responsible supply route).
  The platform ≠ the operating company.
- **Employer / client** — defines the need, decides hiring, owns the work
  relationship and site obligations.
- **Worker** — provides their own information; owns the truth of what they declare.
- **Agency / supply partner / subcontractor** — supplies or manages labour;
  responsible for its own legal standing and the workers it presents.
- **AI system** — drafts, recommendations, and signals only.
- **Future services** — mediation, complaint handling, document-status review,
  remote/in-person work review (documented as **future scope**, not active claims).

## 2. What the platform does NOT take on by itself

Unless explicitly set in a separate contract or through the separate operating
company, the platform is **not** an automatic employer, a document guarantor, a
legal representative, a licensed employment agency, or a guarantor of work quality.

## 3. The AI boundary

AI is a **helper layer** for drafts / recommendations / signals. It is **never** a
legal, document, employment, or final-decision authority. See
[`ai-recommendation-disclaimer.md`](./ai-recommendation-disclaimer.md).

## 4. How this shows up publicly

Public copy speaks about trust, transparency, reputation, document-readiness and
risk reduction — **not** about who is legally responsible for what. The
responsibility split lives in these docs and in any explicit contracts, not in
marketing claims.
