# Partner Risk & Reputation Policy

> How partner trust and risk are handled — honestly, never fabricated. Companion:
> [`partner-and-supply-safety-model.md`](./partner-and-supply-safety-model.md).

## 1. Principles

- Reputation is **earned from real cooperation history**, never invented.
- Risk flags come from **real signals** (missing documents, unclear standing,
  reported problems), never from fabricated data.
- Greater visibility is a **consequence of trust earned**, not a paid shortcut to
  bypass review.
- A partner is **never** publicly labelled in a defamatory way; risk handling is
  internal access control, not a public accusation.

## 2. Risk handling (internal)

- `risk_flagged` → reduced visibility / limited access / review.
- Unverified → `limited_access` or `works_through_approved_route` until reviewed.
- `blocked` only for clear, evidenced cause.
- Customer (client) and worker risk histories are kept as **honest signals** to
  reduce risk for all sides; never fake, never sold, never exposed without basis.

## 3. What we never do

No fake partners, no fake licences, no fake workers, no fake verification, no
purchased reputation, no uncontrolled direct-contact release for unverified
partners. AI never sets a partner's verified/trusted status — that is a human/
operational decision.

## 4. Public framing

Public copy speaks of reputation, transparency and a fair market — never of
"controlling" partners. See [`public-trust-positioning.md`](./public-trust-positioning.md).
